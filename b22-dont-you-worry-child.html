B2.2 listening · English

Don’t You Worry Child

Swedish House Mafia featuring John Martin (2012)

    1

    Before you listen

    Talk about memories and advice, and prepare the key vocabulary.

    Talk in pairs

    1. What is one of your earliest happy memories? Where were you, and who was with you?
    2. What advice or reassurance did adults give you when you were a child? Did it turn out to be true?
    3. Is it ever right to tell a child that everything will be fine when you cannot be sure?
    4. Why do you think so many songs and films look back on childhood?

    Vocabulary

    Match each definition to the correct word or expression.

    2

    Main idea

    Focus on the general story and the feeling of the song.

    3

    Details

    Decide whether each statement is true (T), false (F) or not mentioned (NM).

    4

    Speaking

    Discuss the questions in pairs or small groups.

    Take turns to answer each question. Give reasons and examples, and ask at least one follow-up question before you move on.

    Select a card to mark it as discussed. On a keyboard or clicker, the right arrow key shows the next question.
