Talk Track

A speaking game about sport for groups of two to five. One lap of the track, one lane each.

Who is playing?

3 players (2–5)

How long?

How to play

  1. Use one phone per group and pass it to the next player after each turn.
  2. On your turn, tap the die and move.
  3. On a coloured square, answer the question. Speak for about a minute, and then the person on your left asks you a follow-up question.
  4. On a dark square, follow the instructions on the card.
  5. On a dashed square, learn the expression, use it in a sentence, and move as it says.
  6. The game ends when someone reaches the finish or when the time is up. The player who is furthest along wins.
  7. If someone taps Done by mistake, tap Undo straight away, or tap Last card to see the previous card again.
