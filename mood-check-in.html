Stored on this device only. The timetable and the phrase banks are written into the file itself, so they travel with it to any computer; anything you change here is an override that stays on this browser.

Timetable

One line per class: Day | Name | Level | 09:00-11:00. Level is b21 or b22. Times are optional — add them only when two classes share a day, so it can tell which one you're in.

Add your own phrases

One per line: phrase | emoji | register | definition | mood | idea. Register is reply, easy, core, idiom or slang. Mood is + (positive), = (neutral or mixed) or - (negative). Idea is a one-word family such as tired, busy or happy; two cards from the same family never appear in one grid. The definition is what students see when they flip the card.
