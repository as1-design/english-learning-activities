The three stamps

A rejected sentence is always turned back for one of these reasons.

✗ WRONG MEANING

Grammar fine, but it says something nobody meant — a reason where a contrast belongs.

✗ WRONG STRUCTURE

What follows the gap doesn't fit: a clause where the linker wants a noun, or the other way round.

✗ WRONG REGISTER

Correct English in the wrong clothes: report language in a text, café language in a notice.
